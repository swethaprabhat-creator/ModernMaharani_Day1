## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-end regal Indian couture reimagined for contemporary global sensibilities. The visual direction balances the aristocratic opulence of Hyderabadi heritage—evoking Nizami pearls, zardozi embroidery, and heirloom silks—with editorial minimalism and haute couture digital aesthetics.

### Emotional Response
The interface must instill reverence, exclusivity, and tactile indulgence. Users should feel as though they are stepping into an appointment-only atelier in Banjara Hills, greeted by serene champagne-washed walls, whisper-quiet service, and impeccably curated couture.

### Design Movement
**Minimalist Haute Opulence:** A synthesis of editorial minimalism and tactile luxury.
- Generous, breathing whitespace layered over luminous champagne and warm ivory surfaces.
- Subtle hairline metallic accents reminiscent of gold leaf and antique foil stamping.
- Micro-interactions characterized by slow, deliberate transitions (ease-out cubic bezier curves, gentle opacity fades).
- Absence of harsh shadows, plastic fills, or fast, bouncy animations.

## Colors

The palette is derived from natural heritage dyes, precious metals, and raw tussar silks.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#4A1525` - Royal Maroon):** Anchor color used for critical call-to-actions, hero typography, active states, and focal emblems. Reflects vintage brocade and rich velvet.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059` - Antique Rose Gold):** Used for delicate hairline dividers, focus rings, celebratory accents, monograms, and luxury tier tags.
- **Tertiary (`#B76E79` - Vintage Gulab):** Applied to secondary highlights, delicate notification badges, subtle hover highlights, and curated seasonal capsule categories.
- **Neutral Light (`#FAF8F5` - Raw Ivory & `#F8F4EE` - Pale Champagne):** Base canvas surfaces providing depth without the cold sterility of pure white.
- **Typography Deep Charcoal (`#201B1A`):** High-contrast body copy and descriptive text, preserving legibility while remaining softer than pure `#000000`.

### Color Balance Rules
Keep background surfaces predominantly light (`#FAF8F5` and `#F8F4EE`). Deep maroon (`#4A1525`) should be used sparingly as an editorial punctuation mark, never dominating full screen takeovers unless framing select lookbook covers.

## Typography

The typographical pair establishes high-society editorial gravity via **Playfair Display** while ensuring razor-sharp transaction and utility rendering through **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

### Typographic Hierarchy Rules
- **Editorial Voice:** All collection titles, bespoke bridal headlines, and narrative excerpts employ *Playfair Display* in regular (`400`) or medium (`500`) weights with natural italic expressions used for artisan notes.
- **Commercial & Interface Voice:** Product specifications, dimensions, cart summaries, and monetary figures must strictly use *Plus Jakarta Sans* to preserve crisp legibility across varying viewports.
- **Letter Spacing:** All uppercase micro-labels (`label-sm`, `label-md`) require expanded letter-spacing (`0.1em` to `0.14em`) to reference traditional metal typesetting and boutique packaging tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows an expansive, editorial grid that prioritizes breathing space over visual density, mirroring an upscale physical gallery.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** 1440px max content container, `margin: 4rem` (64px), `gutter: 2rem` (32px).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid container, `margin: 2.5rem` (40px), `gutter: 1.5rem` (24px).
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Fluid container, `margin: 1.5rem` (24px), `gutter: 1rem` (16px).

### Spacing Principles
- **Lavish Whitespace:** Product detail pages and couture showcase galleries utilize `space-xl` between sections to allow bespoke silhouettes to command attention.
- **Vertical Ryhthm:** Component-level paddings strictly conform to half-step increments to maintain an unhurried, calm presentation. Avoid compact UI patterns.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through translucent surface layering and warm ambient glows rather than synthetic, drop-shadowed surfaces.

### Tonal Stratification
- **Ground Tier (Canvas):** `#FAF8F5` base background.
- **Surface Tier (Lookbooks, Item Cards):** `#F8F4EE` or pure `#FFFFFF` overlay cards.
- **Floating Modals & Drawers:** `#FAF8F5` with delicate gold hairlines (`1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.35)`).

### Ambient Diffusions
- **Interactive Hover Depth:** `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(74, 21, 37, 0.06)`. Warm maroon undertone prevents muddy grey casting.
- **Modal Elevation:** `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(43, 24, 16, 0.12)`.
- **Low-Contrast Metallic Borders:** Secondary elevation cues are achieved using crisp `1px` borders in `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.25)` rather than elevated drop shadows.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a strict architectural profile (`roundedness: 0`), referencing heritage bespoke garment boxes, luxury invitation stock, and tailored couture cuts.

### Edge Directives
- **Zero Radius:** Buttons, inputs, product cards, banners, and modals utilize crisp, sharp corners (`0px`).
- **Accent Ribbons & Badges:** Feature clean geometric cuts without pill shaping or soft corner radials.
- **Iconography & Emblems:** Geometric, high-precision lines complementing straight-edge UI boundaries.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Solid deep wine background (`#4A1525`), crisp text in pale champagne (`#F8F4EE`), `0px` border radius, uppercase typography with `letter-spacing: 0.1em`. Hover triggers an ease-in-out transition to rich deep brown (`#2B1810`) with a faint antique gold inset border (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px #C5A059`).
- **Secondary Button:** Transparent fill, `1px solid #C5A059`, typography in `#4A1525`. Hover introduces a wash of soft blush (`#F4DCD6`) at 20% opacity.
- **Tertiary / Link Button:** Pure typography, underlined by a 1px antique gold border offset by 4px.

### Product & Editorial Cards
- **Architecture:** Crisp rectangular image container (`aspect-ratio: 3/4`) showcasing garments with light champagne backgrounds.
- **Border Treatment:** Minimalist `1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.2)` wrapper, providing a picture-frame effect.
- **Badge Ribbons:** Positioned at top-right corner; thin, delicate banner with raw ivory background (`#FAF8F5`), gold foil border, and uppercase micro-type indicating status (`HANDCRAFTED`, `HYDERABAD ATELIER`, `LIMITED RUN`).
- **Product Meta:** Centered alignment. Playfair Display for product nomenclature, followed by lightweight Plus Jakarta Sans for fabrication and price tags.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Inputs:** Floating label aesthetic with a single bottom hairline border (`1px solid rgba(32, 27, 26, 0.25)`) instead of enclosed boxes. On focus, the bottom border shifts to antique gold (`#C5A059`) with a smooth 200ms transition.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Minimalist square geometries (`16px x 16px`). Checkboxes contain a clean diagonal cross or check in `#4A1525`. Radio buttons feature a nested sharp gold square when active.

### Chips & Filters
- **Filter Chips:** Unselected chips feature a subtle champagne background with an ultra-fine border (`1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.3)`). Selected chips turn solid royal maroon (`#4A1525`) with ivory text (`#FAF8F5`).

### Bespoke Couture Components
- **Monogram Seal:** A 1px circular metallic seal (`#C5A059`) framing the brand initials, applied sparingly across authentic certification modals and bespoke concierge drawers.
- **Atelier Swatch Selector:** Miniature square material swatches (brocade, organza, raw silk) framed by a 1px gold hairline upon selection.
